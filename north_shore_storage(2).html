Common questions.

Everything you need to know before your move.

What areas do you serve?

North Shore Storage serves Nassau and Suffolk County on Long Island. Not sure if your address is in our delivery zone? Contact us and we'll confirm.

What if I need the crates for more than a week?

No problem. Your standard rental includes 7 days, and you can add an additional week for $49.99 if you need more time.

Are the crates cleaned between rentals?

Yes. Every crate is cleaned and inspected between rentals so your crates arrive ready for your move.

What happens if a crate is lost or damaged?

Our crates are tracked as part of your rental. Replacement fees may apply to lost or significantly damaged crates; we'll provide the details when you book.

Can I book a same-day delivery?

We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance to help guarantee your preferred delivery window. Need a last-minute rental? Contact us and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

How do I know how many crates I need?

Our packages are based on the size of your move. If you're unsure, contact us with your home size and we'll help you choose the right package.
